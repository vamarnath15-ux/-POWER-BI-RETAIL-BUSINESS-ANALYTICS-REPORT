📊 POWER BI RETAIL BUSINESS ANALYTICS DASHBOARD

🚀 Excited to share one of my recent Power BI practice projects — a dynamic Power BI Sales Dashboard designed to transform raw numbers into meaningful business insights.

🛒 Key Elements of the Dashboard:

🔹 Total Sales & Growth Analysis
🔹 Sales by Category and Segment
🔹 Top 5 Selling Products by Year
🔹 Top 10 Countries by Sales
🔹 Previous Year Total Sales & Growth
🔹 Sales & Profit by Region
🔹 Top 10 Valuable Customers
🔹 Profitability Analysis (Previous Year Profit, Average Profit per Order, Profit Comparison)
🔹 Top Products by Profit, Profit by Segment & Order Priority
🔹 Top 5 Profit-Generating States
🔹 Total Customers, Total Products, and Average Sales per Order

🛠️ Tools & Skills Used:

🟢 Microsoft Power BI
🟢 Data Cleaning & Transformation
🟢 Data Modeling
🟢 DAX & KPI Calculations
🟢 Data Visualization
🟢 Business Intelligence & Insights

💡 Key Learning:

This project helped me understand how raw data can be transformed into an interactive business story that supports better, data-driven decision-making. It also strengthened my practical skills in data analysis, dashboard development, and creating meaningful business insights.
